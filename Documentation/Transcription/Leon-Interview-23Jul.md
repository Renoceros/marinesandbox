### Part 4: Pontoon Planting & Educational Impact

**[00:09:29] Leon**: What else do you want to talk about?

Even now when we plant on the pontoon, it's not so good. Did you feel some of that slime there? So the slime is actually the coral trying to protect itself. Too much contact, whatever, then they start to slime. So that actually causes them stress. 

So the longer we take to plant, the longer the whole process takes, then they get a bit more stressed. So when we plant on the pontoon, then we bring it back down.
