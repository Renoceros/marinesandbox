Frequently Asked Questions

How do I plant and grow my coral fragment?

Clear dead rubble from the survivor fragment on the seabed by flicking it away. Once uncovered, drag your living fragment down onto the sand to anchor it. Over time, your coral will calcify and grow through baby, toddler, and teenage stages.

How do I clean algae or deal with snail pests?

Pull the iridescent sponge tool from the top left corner to brush away algae smothered across coral branches. If coral-eating snails crawl in, simply tap them to smush them, or flick them away.

Is my data saved?

Yes, all your reef progress is saved automatically and locally on your iOS device via Apple SwiftData.
