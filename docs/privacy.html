Support & FAQ Privacy Policy
Privacy Summary: Reefora is built privacy-first. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or monetize your personal data. All gameplay progress is stored purely on your local device.

1. Information We Collect

Reefora does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII):

  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not track your location, browsing habits, or behavioral analytics.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register an account, provide an email address, or create a password to use Reefora.
  • No Third-Party SDKs: The app does not include third-party advertising SDKs, ad trackers, or data broker frameworks.

2. Local Data Storage

Your reef restoration data—including planted coral coordinates, growth stages, algae coverage, and sandbox configuration—is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's local SwiftData framework.

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to back up your iOS device using your own personal iCloud / iTunes backup.

3. Camera and Photo Library Access

If you choose to export or share a 9:16 Reef Share Card, the image is rendered locally on your device and shared directly through the native iOS share sheet. We do not receive, store, or view your shared images.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Reefora is an educational and entertainment product suitable for general audiences and students. Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, we fully comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly to this URL with an updated revision date.
