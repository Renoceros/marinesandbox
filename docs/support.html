Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

How do I care for my coral?

Corals need clean sunlight and protection from pests to thrive. Use the sponge tool to swipe away green algae smothering the coral branches, and tap or flick away predatory snails.

How does Fast Forward work?

Tapping the 10x Fast Forward button in the top right accelerates time for 30 seconds so you can watch your corals advance through their biological growth stages and evaluate ecological balance.

Does Reefora require an internet connection?

No. Reefora works 100% offline. All simulation physics, ecology models, and sandbox state run locally on your device without needing cellular or Wi-Fi connectivity.

How do I restart the sandbox?

You can tap the restart icon in the top right toolbar at any time to reset the sandbox back to the initial cold-open survivor fragment.
